READING THE SURVEY

Evidence, with context.

Each drive is divided into approximately 5 m road segments. The sharpest usable full frame is assessed against the IRC:82-2015 distress taxonomy. A coloured ribbon shows the condition along the route.

Model results need human judgement

The model is a reference, not ground truth. On a labelled benchmark, Stage 1 found about half of distressed frames. Results below the confidence threshold await an expert. A reviewed badge marks human confirmation or correction.

Coverage is not a diagnosis

Surveyed means a segment was covered, including frames the detector chose not to send. Classified means the model answered Normal or Distress. Not covered means no usable frame; it never means a healthy road.

Location and time

Positions are approximate phone GNSS fixes. The selection circle represents one standard deviation (typically ±6 m), not a precise survey boundary. Times are displayed in India Standard Time. MSL altitude is only shown when explicitly supplied.

Reading totals

All observations in this workspace come from drive-video surveys. Distress labels may overlap on one segment, so their percentages must not be added. Per-km coverage bars use mutually exclusive classes.

Ward boundaries

The optional overlay uses historical BBMP ward boundaries for context, not current administrative assignment. Source and attribution are provided with the boundary data.
